DASHBOARD CASE STUDY

ProfitPulse: A clearer view of everyday finances.

A finance dashboard concept for tracking money across key categories.

PRODUCT

Personal finance dashboard

PLATFORM

Desktop web dashboard

FOCUS

Money overview and savings

OVERVIEW

Bring the important money signals into one view.

ProfitPulse is a personal finance dashboard concept that groups earnings, spending, savings, and investments in a single overview. A monthly comparison chart and savings goals add context to the headline figures, while recent transactions keep activity close at hand.

DESIGN APPROACH

Make a quick scan useful, then let people look closer.

  • Lead with the overview. Earnings, spendings, savings, and investment cards summarize the key categories at the top of the dashboard.
  • Add comparison over time. The statistics chart places monthly activity across four categories for a broader view of changes.
  • Show progress toward goals. The savings panel pairs the total with category progress bars and target amounts.
  • Keep activity visible. Recent transactions sit beneath the main metrics, with an app download card alongside the dashboard content.
TAKEAWAY

A compact dashboard organized around overview, trends, goals, and activity.

The design brings together four complementary views of personal finances: current category totals, month-by-month comparisons, savings progress, and recent transactions. Together, these give the page a clear scan path from summary to supporting detail.
