CASE STUDY

FitFuel: Find the gear. Choose your fit. Keep moving.

An activewear storefront that carries shoppers from collection discovery to cart.

DESIGN FOCUS

E-commerce experience

JOURNEY

Discover → browse → cart

SCREENS

Storefront · listing · product · cart

PROJECT BRIEF

Bring the full gear-shopping journey into one connected storefront.

PROJECT TYPE

Activewear e-commerce storefront

DESIGNED FOR

People shopping for training and everyday activewear

DESIGN FOCUS

Collection discovery, product selection, and cart clarity

Design question How might a fitness storefront help shoppers find relevant gear, understand product options, and move confidently toward checkout?

OVERVIEW

Premium activewear, designed for performance and everyday life.

FitFuel brings apparel, training gear, and fitness accessories into one shopping experience. The Figma flow starts with collection discovery, then lets shoppers narrow the catalogue, compare product details, choose options, and review their cart before checkout.

The landing page also introduces team gear and FitFuel Tracker, extending the storefront into group orders and workout tracking.

01 / DISCOVER

Start with collections, then surface reasons to explore.

The storefront opens with “Fuel Your Fitness Journey” and a direct Shop Collections action. Men, Women, and Kids categories sit alongside new arrivals and best sellers, giving shoppers several entry points into the catalogue.

Further down, the page promotes team gear and FitFuel Tracker. Its feature list calls out workout logging and analytics, personalized training plans, and member rewards.

02 / BROWSE

Give shoppers useful ways to narrow the range.

The product listing places category navigation and filters beside the product grid. Shoppers can sort by newest and filter by product type, price, fit, size, color, and discount. Product cards keep imagery, names, prices, and ratings visible while scanning.

03 / EVALUATE

Make the product choice clear before it reaches the cart.

The EnduraZip Hoodie page brings together its rating and reviews, price, color, size, and quantity controls with an Add to Cart action. Supporting details call out free returns within 30 days, shipping in 1–2 business days, and secure checkout.

Description, Features and Fit, Material and Care, and Customer reviews provide separate places for shoppers to inspect product information.

04 / REVIEW

Keep the order, delivery progress, and next step together.

The cart drawer confirms the selected item and options, then adds relevant recommendations. A delivery progress indicator shows how the order compares with the free-delivery threshold, while the summary separates subtotal, delivery fee, and total.

From here, shoppers can apply a discount code, proceed to checkout, or continue shopping.

END-TO-END FLOW

From “Shop Collections” to a checkout-ready order.

The screens connect merchandising to purchase: explore a collection, narrow the product list, review a product and its options, then inspect the cart and continue to checkout. Team gear and the Tracker promotion add wider brand destinations around that core shopping path.
