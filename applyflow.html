CASE STUDY

Applyflow: Keeping track of your job search, without the stress of losing track.

ROLE

Product Designer

INDUSTRY

HR Tech

DURATION

8 Weeks

Overview

Applyflow is a personal product design project in the HR Tech and Career Management space. My focus was on designing the application tracking experience, particularly the interactions users need when moving from an interview to a job offer.

The Problem

Keeping track of multiple job applications can become difficult when each opportunity is at a different stage. Users need to manage interview schedules, update application statuses, and remember important offer details, so I explored how these tasks could be brought together in one connected experience.

The Design Goal

I wanted to make job tracking feel straightforward without forcing users to complete every task immediately. This meant giving them control over their application updates while keeping important information and unfinished actions visible.

The Solution

1. Managing Interview Details

Users can view their scheduled interviews and long-press an interview card to edit or delete its details. I kept these actions within a contextual menu so users can access them when needed without adding more permanent buttons to the card.

2. Updating Application Status

Users can change an application's status as they progress through the hiring process, including moving from Interview to Offer, Rejected, or Withdrawn. The status selection screen shows the current status before the user chooses another one, and selecting Offer leads directly to the offer details step.

Final thoughts

Working on Applyflow has given me an opportunity to think beyond individual screens and focus on how different interactions fit together.

For me, it's not just about tracking applications. It's about helping people stay organized through a process that can sometimes feel uncertain and overwhelming. There's still more to explore, but this is a step towards making that experience a little easier.
